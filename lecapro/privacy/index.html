Privacy Policy

Application: Leca Pro
Developer: OneBit Logic
Effective Date: November 23, 2026

Summary: Leca Pro is a paid, offline application with no ads and no tracking. We do not collect, store, or transmit your personal data to external servers.

1. Information We Do Not Collect

OneBit Logic respects your privacy. Leca Pro operates entirely offline on your device. We do not collect, log, store, share, or sell any personally identifiable information (PII), usage analytics, location data, or device identifiers.

  • No user accounts or registration required.
  • No third-party advertising networks or ad SDKs.
  • No third-party analytics or tracking tools.
  • No cloud storage or external data transmission.

2. Device Permissions & Local Processing

To provide its core camera and image-processing functionality, Leca Pro requests access to device permissions such as Camera and Storage / Media. All photos, RAW/DNG files, and app settings are processed and stored exclusively on your local device (or external storage you explicitly select) under your full control. Nothing is ever uploaded to OneBit Logic or any third party.

3. Payments and Purchases

All purchases of Leca Pro are processed exclusively through the Google Play Store. OneBit Logic does not have access to your credit card details, billing address, or other financial information. Payment processing is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

4. Crash Reports and Diagnostics

If you have enabled diagnostic sharing in your Android system settings, Google Play may automatically provide us with anonymized crash logs and basic device information (such as Android version and device model) to help us fix bugs and improve stability. These reports do not contain personal information.

5. Children's Privacy

Because Leca Pro does not collect any personal information from any user, we do not knowingly collect personal data from children under the age of 13 (or the applicable age of digital consent in your jurisdiction).

6. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date. Continued use of Leca Pro after changes are posted constitutes your acceptance of the updated policy.
